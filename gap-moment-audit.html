The Gap Moment Audit™

A typical CRNA shift holds 30–40 natural transitions — small seams between one thing and the next. Most pass unused. A few are quietly expensive. This is a map of your shift: mark the transitions you recognize, rate the spike, and flag two or three to train. Take it home.

The reframe: a Gap Moment isn't downtime to fill — it's a rep you already own. You're not adding a practice to your day; you're making conscious a moment that's already there. The post-induction, pre-incision seam — 30 to 60 seconds after the patient goes under and before the surgeon begins — is the one only decades in the room teach you to see.
