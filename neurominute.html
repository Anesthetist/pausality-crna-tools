Paced breathing near ~5.5 breaths/min (≈0.1 Hz) is the resonance frequency at which heart-rate variability is maximized (Lehrer & Gevirtz, 2014; Lehrer et al., 2000). A reflection-and-practice tool, not medical treatment. If you have dizziness, are pregnant, dehydrated, or have a neurovascular condition, breathe normally rather than pacing.
